### Background & Research
I earned my PhD in Physics from the University of Michigan in 2019. During my graduate research, I spent five years repurposing data from the Dark Energy Survey to study the outer Solar System beyond Neptune — a region known as the **Kuiper Belt**. 

Because the Kuiper Belt has remained largely untouched since the formation of the Solar System, studying these icy bodies and their orbits offers a window into what the early Solar System looked like 4.5 billion years ago. My work led to the discovery and characterization of several distant objects, including a **new dwarf planet candidate**.

### Science Communication & Outreach
I discovered my passion for science communication in graduate school and have been doing it ever since!

* **Reporting & Writing:** Summer 2019 AAAS Mass Media Fellow for the *Indianapolis Star*; former writer for *Astrobites*, *The Planetary Society*, and *Natural History Magazine*.
* **Training & Mentorship:** Co-led workshops with UMich’s RELATE organization, training over 500 researchers to communicate complex science effectively.
* **Events & Community:** Organized major SciComm conferences including *ComSciCon* and *Science Talk*, with a focus on improving inclusivity in STEM spaces.
