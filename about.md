			<h2>Hey, I'm Stephanie!</h2>
		</header>
		<p>I’m a science communicator, planetary astronomer, and content strategist (not necessarily in that order). I love finding ways to make complicated science approachable and fun.</p>

<p>Today, I’m the Astronomy Content Strategist for NSF–DOE Rubin Observatory, where I get to do what I love for a living: tell stories about astronomy, help people understand how we explore the Universe, and occasionally get very excited about rocks in space.</p>

<p>When I’m not talking about astronomy, you’ll probably find me reading an enormous fantasy book, planning my next mountain hiking adventure with my hubby, or hanging out with my cat.</p>

In 2019, I was a science reporter at The Indianapolis Star through the AAAS Mass Media Fellowship. Now, I get to widely share the incredible science made possible by Rubin Observatory!</p>
