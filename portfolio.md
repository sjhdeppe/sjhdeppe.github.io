## Other Public Engagement & Outreach

*   **Social Media Content Strategist:** (2018–Present) Communicated science via my personal social media account,[@SpaceSciSteph](https://www.twitter.com/SpaceSciSteph). Curated accounts for Unique Scientists, #BlackInSciCommWeek, @AGU_SciComm, and @astrotweeps.
*   **Curious Science Writers:** Mentor (2020) for high school students developing science writing skills.
*   **Portal to the Public:** Science Communication Fellow (2017–2018). Developed hands-on activities to demonstrate Solar System scale to families at the Detroit Zoo and UMich Museum of Natural History.
*   **Dark Energy Survey EPO:** (2016–2018) Facilitated astronomy outreach efforts for 400+ scientists, answering questions for UK school children via *Dark Bites*, and publicizing results.
*   **Society for Women in Physics (SWIP):** (2015–2019) Highlighted minority physicists through departmental posters and presented physics demonstrations to middle school students.
